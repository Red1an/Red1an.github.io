University projects

Labs I treat as small research projects rather than homework.

Simulation modeling · 2026

Giant hogweed: can it be stopped?

A cellular automaton on a 150×150 map with forests, meadows, a river, roads and villages. Hogweed grows, blooms and spreads seeds, while a control service with a limited budget tries to stop it.

  • A custom model: a soil seed bank, seeds carried along rivers and roads, competition with native vegetation.
  • 6 control methods — mowing, herbicide, black film, sowing grass, goats — and strategies under a yearly budget.
  • Batch runs over 40 years: left alone, hogweed covers 20% of the land by year ~26; starting control in year 5 instead of year 12 makes winning 7–11 times cheaper.
